## Key Learnings

1. **Context depth = output quality.** A detailed resume + JD produce precise gap analysis. We must do it, when applying for our dream job.

2. **ATS optimisation is pattern matching.** AI can systematically compare a JD's keyword list against a resume and flag every mismatch, GraphQL, React Router, Node.js projects, in seconds. Rejection rate get higher if mismatch count is higher.

3. **Best proof points are buried.** The two biggest wins on this resume, the API and ETL transformations, were written as project bullets, not career headlines. Your most impressive work is likely hidden the same way. Excavate it.

4. **Leadership is underrepresented in most senior resumes.** Leading onshore/offshore teams, running sprint planning, governing Jenkins pipelines, and mentoring developers all appear as supporting detail in the original resume. At Principal level, leadership is the primary product.

5. **Certifications are table stakes, not differentiators.** Azure Fundamentals, Data, and AI certs clear HR filters. But at Principal level, demonstrated applied cloud work differentiates. Applied beats certified every time.

6. **The mid-career MTech from BITS Pilani is underutilised.** A Master's in Data Science completed in 2021–2023 signals long-term investment in growth and relevance. It should appear earlier and more prominently — especially when targeting Emerging-Tech roles where ML/AI literacy is increasingly expected.

7. **Your next title is determined by how you tell your current story.** "Staff Engineer managing cross-functional teams" reads differently than "Technical authority driving architecture decisions and building engineering capability." Same reality. One gets you a lateral move; the other gets you promoted.
