Nutrition Sources & Methodology

How the numbers are derived

Transparent assumptions behind every calculation.

Energy (BMR & TDEE)

Mifflin–St Jeor equation for resting metabolism, scaled by an activity multiplier (1.2 sedentary → 1.9 very active) to estimate Total Daily Energy Expenditure.

Macronutrient Targets

Protein at 1.6 g/kg body weight, fat at 25% of energy, carbohydrate as the remainder, and fibre at 14 g per 1000 kcal — common general-population ratios.

Micronutrient Targets

Daily values modeled on ICMR-NIN RDA (India, 2020) and US NIH / IOM Dietary Reference Intakes, adjusted by sex. Sodium is treated as an upper guide (~2300 mg).

Food Composition

Per-100g nutrient values are approximations modeled on the Indian Food Composition Tables (IFCT 2017, NIN Hyderabad) and USDA FoodData Central. Cooking, brands & recipes shift real values.
